# UniCredit – Banco BPM M&A Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the proposed merger between UniCredit and Banco BPM, focusing on balance sheet structure, profitability, interest rate sensitivity, liquidity risk and post-merger market implications within the Italian banking sector.

The analysis evaluates the strategic and financial rationale of the transaction through a combination of financial statement analysis, leverage-adjusted duration gap analysis, liquidity stress testing and regulatory capital considerations.

The project was developed as part of a banking and financial intermediation case study with a focus on banking risk management, merger implications and balance sheet optimization.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of the analysis were:

- Evaluate the financial strength and profitability of UniCredit and Banco BPM
- Assess the impact of interest rate risk on both institutions
- Analyze liquidity risk under stressed market conditions
- Examine the implications of forced asset liquidation scenarios
- Evaluate post-merger market concentration and competitive positioning
- Assess leverage, funding structure and capital management strategies

---

## Key Areas of Analysis

### Part I – Bank Balance Sheet Analysis

- ECB reserve requirement compliance
- Profitability analysis (ROA, ROE, profit margin)
- Asset utilization and operational efficiency
- Impact of non-performing loans on net worth
- Equity multiplier and leverage strategies
- Market concentration and post-merger competitive positioning

### Part II – Interest Rate Risk

- Leverage-adjusted duration gap analysis
- Interest rate sensitivity of assets and liabilities
- Impact of monetary policy shifts on bank equity
- Balance sheet exposure to rate volatility
- Asset-liability management considerations
- Post-merger integration implications from a treasury perspective

### Part III – Liquidity Risk

- Liquidity stress-testing scenarios
- Forced asset liquidation analysis
- Fire-sale pricing implications
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) assessment
- Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) analysis
- Funding structure and short-term liquidity resilience
